Vazhikatti

A Web SaaS Which Reduce The Situation Of Patients And Visitors Relying On Manual Direction Inquires At Hospital Desks Which Results In Reduced Navigation Time.

Video Walkthrough Of Vazhikatti

Overview

Vazhikatti is a web-based, indoor navigation SaaS platform designed for healthcare facilities to reduce visitor direction confusion and lower the time for navigation by guiding the patients to their exact location. By leveraging QR code scanning and dynamic web based navigation, the platform guides patients and visitors directly to hospital departments, rooms, and facilities as per their desired location. The system reduces information desk crowding, cuts down staff interruptions, and equips hospital administrators with real-time operational traffic analytics..
